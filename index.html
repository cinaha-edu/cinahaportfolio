    About Me

    I am a UI/UX designer and an aspiring front end developer. As an aspiring front end developer, I coded my entire website on Visual Studio Code! I want to focus on creating engaging and user-focused that is supported by UX research and design principles.

    I was born in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, and was raised in North Philly! I am of Cantonese descent and speak Cantonese fluently. I speak English, Cantonese, and Spanish, and I'm learning Korean and Mandarin.

    In my free time, I love traveling, playing the violin, and gaming! I have been to seven countries: Vietnam, France, Canada, South Korea, China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan! I'm a self-taught violinist and play with Drexel University's Symphony Orchestra. My favorite games are League of Legends, Minecraft, and WildRift.
