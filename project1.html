    • Asian American Women's Coalition Website Redesign

    Role

    UX Designer (Solo)

    Timeline

    Sept 2025 - Jun 2026

    Project Type

    UI/UX Research & Design

    Tools

    Figma

    Overview

    Over 30 weeks, I learned how to conduct a case study through UX research and usability testing, low-fidelity Figma protoytypes, and high-fidelity Figma prototypes in my UXID classes. I conducted my case study on the Asian American Women's Coalition website, a Philadelphia-native non-profit organization, and aimed to increase engagement and outreach.

    Phase 01

    UX Research

    Initial Research

    I conducted a quick, inital research to understand AAWC (Asian American Women's Coalition) missions, objectives, and history to best assist my user research and design choices.

    First Learning About AAWC & Their Impact

    Business Model Canvas

    Then, I researched and created a Business Model Canvas to further understand AAWC's non-profit practices and how it intertwines with their branding.

    Researching AAWC's Affiliations & Non-Profit Background

    User Research

    I interviewed past AAWC alumni and scholarship recipients to build accurate User Personas, User Journey Maps, And User Empathy Maps.

    AAWC Alumni & Member Interviews

    User Personas

    I used the information I gathered from the User Interviews of local, Asian American, Philadelphian women. Additionally, AAWC's website includes information about every scholarship winner and their Philadelphia upbringing.

    User Personas Based On User Interviews & Common AAWC Scholarship Recipients

    User Empathy Map & User Journey Map

    I used User Interviews and every other piece of research to conjure a map of a user navigating AAWC's outdated website.

    The Feelings Of A User Navigating AAWC's Website

    Obstacles Of A AAWC Website User

    Key Takeaways

    Benefits

      - Long legacy of giving back to the Philadelphia community

      - Scholarship applications are easy to access

    Obstacles

    - Website isn't engaging at all, and AAWC members and alumni report never using the website outside of scholarship applications

    - No reason for users to revist the website, leading to 0 foot traffic

    - Website UI/Design is outdated and isn't properly updated

    Design Priorities

    1. Update the website and seriously rework the content, modernize the interface

    2. Show the date Scholarship Applications open, the time frame, and document requirements

    3. Cut out older information, archives, and names. Feels like a lot of content but lack of substance

    4. Create incentives for members/non-members to visit the website like event/workshop sign-ups.

    5. Add more events/any events to the calendar and keep it consistently updated

    Phase 02

    Low-Fidelity Design

    Problem

    What is the main design problem?

    People who navigate AAWC's website sees no reason to revisit the site. With a hard-to-find calendar that is 5+ clicks into the website and a scholarship page that only houses a single google form link, I made it my priority to rework these two main paths that a user expects to be engaging on every website.

    No Interactive Element On Home Page

    Only A Underlined Link To Follow To External Google Form

    Solution

    My Low-Fidelity Solution

    I added events to the main home page instead of leaving it in an outdated calendar that is 5+ clicks away from the user and their goal. Furthermore, the scholarship page is now interactive and shows you information about the scholarship without sending you to an unknown external source.

    Interactive Element On Home Page

    Information & Interactions On The Scholarship Page

    Phase 03

    High-Fidelity Design

    Redesign

    What was my low-fidelity design lacking?

    While I had added new interactive elements for users to have a reason to revisit the AAWC website, the design was overall very in line with the original outdated AAWC website because I hadn't broadened my horizon and took all pictures and inspirations from the original website.

    For my high-fidelity redesign, I focused on keeping the essence of AAWC's branding, like the purples, soft elements, and informative pages, but added a modern twist on the design to revamp the website and give users something exciting to look at.

    Sleek & Modern Home Page Without Poor Quality Photos

    New Scholarship Navigation System That Has Micro-Interactions & Is Informative

    Learning

    Reflection

    This project was 30 weeks of hard work oevr the span of one year that drilled UI/UX design and research fundamentals into my head. While I look back on my designs with even more solutions and redesigns, I am proud of the amount of research, tweaking, and genuine analysis that I put into this organization and website. It was a cause that hit close to home and something that was local to me as well. Going forward, I hope to build upon my fundamentals and produce evolved redesigns at a quicker pace and at an enterprise-level.
